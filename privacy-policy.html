FLUX BLOCKS – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Who we are

FLUX BLOCKS is a mobile puzzle game published by Stargazer Labs ("we", "us"). Contact: halis6c@gmail.com.

Information we do not collect

The game has no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of our own. We do not collect your name, email address, location or any other personal information.

Information stored on your device

Your best score, settings (such as sound) and saved game are stored locally on your device. This data is never sent to us. It is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows two kinds of ads: optional rewarded ads, which are shown only if you choose to watch one (for example, to continue a game), and short full-screen ads that may appear between games. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, a third-party service. AdMob may collect and use data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information and ad interactions, in order to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud. We do not receive this data. Details are in Google's Privacy Policy and at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Consent and your choices

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the game shows a consent form before ads are requested. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Children

FLUX BLOCKS is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data retention and deletion

We hold no personal data about you. Data stored on your device can be deleted by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the game. For data held by Google, please use Google's own privacy controls.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it last changed.
